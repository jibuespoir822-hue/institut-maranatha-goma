Join the Institut Maranatha family for the upcoming academic year in Goma.

Required Documents (DRC Standards)

  • Copy of the student's Birth Certificate (*Acte de naissance*).
  • Previous two years' official school report cards (*Bulletins scolaires*).
  • Certificate of Good Conduct (*Certificat de bonne vie et mœurs*) for transfer students.
  • 4 recent passport-size photographs.
  • Medical fitness certificate (*Certificat d'aptitude physique*).
