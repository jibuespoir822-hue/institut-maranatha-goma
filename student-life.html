Student Life & Activities

Building character, teamwork, and leadership outside the classroom.

Youth Leadership

Pathfinder & Adventurer Clubs

Our active Adventist youth clubs teach outdoor survival skills, discipline, first aid, civic responsibility, and teamwork through camps and weekend service projects.

Music & Worship

Maranatha Youth Choir

Music is central to Adventist heritage. Students develop vocal harmony and musical talent, performing at school ceremonies, graduations, and local church services in Goma.

Health & Fitness

Sports & Inter-School Tournaments

Promoting physical health and fair play through football, basketball, volleyball, and athletics competitions across Goma.

Public Speaking

Bilingual Debate & Science Clubs

Students sharpen their critical thinking and public speaking in both French and English, preparing them for regional academic competitions.
